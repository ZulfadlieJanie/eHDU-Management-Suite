📌 Operational Overview & Strategic Scope

The e-HDU Management Suite is the centralized digital management portal engineered specifically for the Haemodialysis Unit (HDU), Department of Nephrology, Sarawak General Hospital.

While the Sarawak Nephrology Integrated Database System (SNIDS) serves as our primary statewide platform dedicated to direct patient care, clinical hemodialysis charting, and workload analytics, the e-HDU Management Suite acts as its vital operational counterpart. This system is purpose-built to govern unit-level infrastructure—centralizing water treatment engineering surveillance, asset reliability, consumables supply chain integrity, and clinical audit readiness into a unified paperless workflow.

All clinical nursing personnel, technical operators, and hospital support services (BEMS) are required to execute shift logs, maintenance records, and regulatory verifications through their designated modules.

🌱 Strategic Direction & Sustainability
Towards a Green Healthcare Facility: Phasing out manual paper logs, bound registry books, and printed work orders in alignment with MOH sustainable development goals, reducing environmental footprint and physical record storage overhead.
Building a Smart HDU: Establishing standardized digital datasets to enable automated predictive alerts, real-time consumable burn-rate monitoring, and machine health telemetry.
Standardized Regulatory Audit Readiness: Providing instantaneous, export-ready data trails for CKAPS, MOH technical surveillance, and external hospital accreditation audits.
